How do I get a key?
  1. On GitHub, click your profile picture, then Settings, then Developer settings (bottom of the left sidebar).
  2. Choose Personal access tokens, then Fine-grained tokens, then Generate new token.
  3. Name it "Handmade admin" and pick an expiration (1 year is fine).
  4. Under Repository access, choose Only select repositories and pick handmade.
  5. Under Permissions, set Contents to Read and write.
  6. Click Generate token, copy it, and paste it above. This browser remembers it until you sign out.
