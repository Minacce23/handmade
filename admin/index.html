How do I get a key?
  1. On GitHub, click your profile picture, then Settings, then Developer settings (bottom of the left sidebar).
  2. Choose Personal access tokens, then Fine-grained tokens, then Generate new token.
  3. Name it "Handmade admin" and pick an expiration (1 year is fine).
  4. Under Repository access, choose Only select repositories and pick handmade.
  5. Under Permissions, set Contents to Read and write.
  6. Click Generate token, copy it, and paste it above. This browser remembers it until you sign out.
Handmade admin View site

Drag a photo onto another project to move it. The first photo in each project is its cover. When a project has more than one category, use the small labels under each photo to say which craft it shows. Hide keeps a photo off the site, × takes it out of the project. Nothing goes live until you click Save changes.

Loose photos

Photos that are not in any project. They stay saved but don't show on the site. Drag one onto a project, use its Move menu, or click Delete to remove it for good.

Rotate or crop

Drag inside the box to move it and drag the corners to resize. Your original photo is kept in Loose photos in case you want it back.
